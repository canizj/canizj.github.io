01 / AI & AUTOMATION

Mr. E.

AI-assisted architecture for mortgage recruiting.

A proof of concept for candidate research, NMLS identity verification, and CRM data unification, with recruiters reviewing the results before any update.

THE CHALLENGE

Optimizing data aggregation for recruitment.

Mortgage recruiting data is spread across production reports, NMLS records, and the CRM. Comparing it across tabs takes time and leaves room for manual entry errors.

I designed an AI-assisted workflow to reconcile that evidence in one place and give recruiters explicit control over CRM updates.

THE WORKFLOW

Research. Verify. Review.

01 / DATA EXTRACTION & VERIFICATION

Integrating distributed intelligence.

Bring browser research, figures from mortgage production data platforms like Model Match and MMI, and NMLS identity evidence into a structured view.

02 / QUALITY ASSURANCE & SYSTEM UPDATE

Maintaining operational integrity.

Generate standardized CRM notes locally, then let the recruiter validate, edit, and approve the proposed update.

THREE RECRUITING WORKFLOWS

AI-assisted workflows for recruiting decisions.

Try the three workflows I designed to consolidate research, surface corrections, and explain candidate fit. Each simulation uses fictional or sanitized records.

01 / CANDIDATE DOSSIER GENERATION

Mortgage Intelligence Agent

Consolidate candidate and company research from the CRM, NMLS, and a production data platform, reconcile identity and production figures, and generate a standardized recruiting profile.

02 / CRM CONVERSATION ANALYSIS

Quality Check

Review the latest CRM conversation, tags, contact details, and employment. Trace findings to their sources and preview proposed corrections or follow-up actions.

03 / CANDIDATE FIT EVALUATION

Leader Matching

Rank five partner campaigns by role, geography, licensing context, production, and recruiting priorities. Choose a leader to see what fits, what holds the match back, and up to three alternatives.

THE RESULT

Independent Chrome extension proof of concept.

I built and tested a Chrome extension that combines Model Match production data with NMLS identity evidence in an editable CRM note. I also used Gemini with MMI to research and reconcile production figures for the same workflow.

The documented validation covered the read-only research and draft workflow.

How it connects to any CRM

Follow Up Boss was the CRM used in the proof of concept. To connect another CRM, authenticate with its API and map its contact IDs, fields, activity records, and notes to a common format. The same research and review workflow uses that format to prepare a draft and preview changes.

After recruiter approval, the connector maps the approved changes back to the destination fields and submits them through the CRM API. This approach can work with any CRM that exposes the required read and write operations and grants the appropriate permissions.
