01 / AI & AUTOMATION

Mr. E.

AI-assisted mortgage recruiting, from candidate research to recruiter-approved CRM notes.

Working proof of concept · Chrome extension · Recruiter-approved posting

THE CHALLENGE

From research
to actionable business intelligence.

Mortgage candidate research spans production data, identity checks, and CRM documentation. Mr. E brings those steps into a structured workflow with a person approving the final post.

I designed and built an AI-assisted Chrome extension integrating Model Match and Follow Up Boss, with three-point NMLS identity verification, local fixed-template note assembly, copy and edit controls, and explicit approval before posting.

THE WORKFLOW

Research. Verify. Review. Post.

01 / RESEARCH & VERIFY

Bring candidate research together

Bring candidate information together from multiple sources using Google search and browser research, alongside Model Match data and NMLS identity verification.

02 / REVIEW & POST

Keep the recruiter in control

Assemble a consistent note locally, allow copying and editing, and require human approval before saving it to Follow Up Boss.

THREE RECRUITING WORKFLOWS

Three ways to support
recruiting decisions.

I built and used three AI-assisted recruiting workflows in Gemini, combining browser-based research, information extraction, cross-tab comparison, summaries, and action items. I then built a Chrome extension proof of concept connecting candidate research to recruiter-approved CRM note posting.

01 / CANDIDATE RESEARCH

A structured recruiting profile

Combine candidate and company research from three sources, reconcile identity and production information, and prepare a consistent CRM-ready note.

02 / CRM QUALITY REVIEW

Review the candidate conversation

Analyze candidate conversation threads, contact details, and tags; summarize relevant context and identify corrections and follow-up actions for recruiter review.

03 / LEADER FIT ANALYSIS

Explain the strongest matches

Produce the top five leader fits and explain unsuitable matches using candidate qualifications, location, licensing, production criteria, recruiting priorities, and exclusions.

THE RESULT

Working proof
of concept.

Built a Chrome extension that saved a structured mortgage recruiting note directly to Follow Up Boss after recruiter approval.

The workflow combined Model Match production data, NMLS identity verification, editable note formatting, and CRM posting in one experience.
