    How we researched and validated the matches

    The Phase 1 sanitized study defines five cases and eight partner campaign rows. The audit compares candidate role, county and metro, production windows, product mix and employer restrictions. Protected employer rules take priority. Candidate totals are not proof of self-generated production in a different period.

    This prototype compares eight sanitized campaigns locally. The ranking prioritizes eligible or pending candidates, then geography, role and product mix. A high rank can still be blocked or pending evidence. In a connected workflow, CRM provides the candidate baseline, NMLS supplies licensure and registered location, MMI supplies production evidence, and the Partner Sheet supplies campaign criteria and column AL protections.
