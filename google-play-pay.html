01 / Portfolio Structure & Overview

Operations and project leadership at TELUS

In operations and project leadership across Guatemala and Las Vegas, I led teams and built site-wide process improvements centered on Google Play and Google Pay, while also providing cross-vertical operational support for Google Workspace (formerly G Suite), Google Fi, Google One, and Fiber. My process-innovation foundation began in Guatemala on the G Suite Trials Guardian initiative, where I audited abruptly canceled customer trials and identified that churn was driven by unhandled technical setup and configuration blockers rather than product limitations. I established a specialized Trial Recovery workflow to re-engage canceled accounts, resolve configuration issues, and transition customers to ongoing technical support—lifting floor-wide trial-to-paid conversion by 3% and earning monthly Top Performer recognition.

Continuing in operations leadership in Las Vegas, I led Google Play teams and supported cross-product Universal support queues through the 2017 campaign wind-down before being selected to help reopen and relaunch the Google Play operation. During the relaunch, I developed and scaled a multi-site survey transfer pilot for Google Play, led a Lean Six Sigma identity verification redesign for Google Pay, and built site-wide quality and coaching frameworks.

02 / Google Play

Team Supreme and the Voice of the Customer

Voice of the CustomerSurvey transferPilot scaling

I created and led Team Supreme, a five-person pilot that gave more customers the chance to share feedback after a support interaction. The goal was to make survey handoffs more consistent and give the site a clearer view of the customer experience. I grouped the barriers into process, policy, product, and agent execution. The review covered dropped-call callbacks, knowledge-base exemptions, inconsistent guidance on third-party transfers, and a reporting issue where email transfers could count as phone surveys.

The pilot also brought departments together to review recurring issues and work through fixes jointly. That gave teams a way to resolve issues that had previously been passed between them.

03 / Google Pay

Lean Six Sigma and in-app identity verification

Lean Six SigmaProcess mappingRoot-cause analysis

I mapped the automated verification journey from customer data entry through identity confirmation. I audited cases, interviewed agents across support tiers and other stakeholders, and used the Five Whys to trace recurring errors. Failed verification could prevent customers from using their cards for about two days and drive spikes in calls. Customers who tried to make payments overnight also faced a Tier 2 coverage gap.

The project took about six months from investigation through implementation. I redrew the process and proposed guided in-app steps, including taking a photo, so customers could actively confirm their identity. I submitted the revised map and worked with stakeholders on the final changes.

04 / SOPs & Knowledge Ecosystem

SOPs, product pilots, and coaching

Share product knowledge

I created The Professor to recognize agents who taught customers how to use product features during support calls. I also designed certificates for top performers. I proposed product feature workshops and refresher sessions where Quality Assurance and team leads could revisit app features and knowledge-base guidance with agents who needed extra support.

Test features with Google and learn from customers

In Movie Club and Book Club, we watched a movie or read a book with Google staff while trying product features and giving feedback on what worked and what needed refinement before release. I also ran site pilots brought to us by the vendor manager and managed the 2019 TELUS initiatives and pilots document.

For High-Value User Customer Engagement, I had team members play popular games and join player communities to understand customers firsthand. The program included participation rewards. For the Device Lab, I managed a $10,000 hardware budget and selected Android phones from different manufacturers and TV devices so teams could test features directly on the floor.

Build site-wide coaching and quality practices

In 2017, drawing on an approved operational support model from the Guatemala site, I piloted a Point of Contact (POC) leadership-development workflow with three self-trained team members—delegating KPI tracking, case audits, and frontline floor support under my supervisory oversight—before presenting a formal 10-slide site-wide business case. Framed as a KPI-accountable upgrade to the prior Subject Matter Expert (SME) role, the proposal designated one POC per team (budgeted at 3 hours of dedicated off-queue coaching and analysis time, or $180 per week per team, paired with locked CSAT and QA incentives) to cover Team Leader absences, audit daily customer sentiment and dissatisfaction dashboards, track authentication and survey compliance, issue weekly performance reports, hold cross-team calibration meetings, and conduct weekly live side-by-side coaching for three assigned bottom-quartile team members. Candidate eligibility required completing internal leadership-development modules, passing an Operations Manager evaluation, maintaining zero corrective actions in the trailing three months, keeping monthly absenteeism below 5%, and sustaining at least 85% schedule adherence. Although the initial Google Play campaign closed in 2017 prior to site-wide rollout, the site operated a revamped SME structure when I returned to help reopen the program.

I also created the CC 2.0 call checklist so agents could consistently cover the steps QA assessed.

After earning top-performing Team Lead recognition, I became the first Coaching Champion at the site. I ran a special project with the lead account supervisor and coached about 50 agents per week across shifts, including overnight. I focused on agents in the bottom quartile and used a SWOT-based approach to identify strengths to build on, recurring quality gaps, opportunities for improvement, and barriers to progress. I used those findings to guide call scripts, workshops, individual coaching, and performance improvement plans.
