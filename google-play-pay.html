03 / Business Operations

Google Play & Pay.

Customer Insights & Operations Improvement

As an Operations Team Supervisor at TELUS, I served as project lead for site improvement initiatives across Google Play and Google Pay.

01 / Google Play

From a five-person pilot to floor-wide rollout.

I created and led a pilot to improve post-contact survey transfers. I reviewed callback procedures, survey exemptions, and cross-channel reporting. The review identified cases transferred by email that could be counted as phone surveys.

I organized the review around four sources of missed transfers: process, policy, product, and agent execution. That meant looking at dropped-call callbacks, knowledge-base exemptions, how the reporting system classified surveys, and whether agents had current guidance for third-party transfers. The aim was to make survey handoffs more consistent while keeping the customer experience central.

Survey transfer rates rose from about 37% to 67–68% in the five-person pilot and from about 42% to 57% during the floor-wide rollout.

In a separate dashboard snapshot, my team recorded 75% chat customer satisfaction across roughly 1,200 survey responses, the highest chat score among the five teams shown.

02 / Google Pay

Lean Six Sigma process improvement.

I led this Google Pay process improvement project to earn my Lean Six Sigma Yellow Belt. I used Pareto analysis and case audits to investigate drivers of customer dissatisfaction, mapped the identity verification workflow, and identified opportunities to streamline automated verification.

I mapped the verification steps from initial setup through escalation and reviewed where automated verification created extra work. I proposed changes to simplify those steps and used case categories to distinguish verification friction from issues that required a different support response.

Case timestamps also revealed a gap in overnight Tier 2 support. I worked with Quality Assurance, Workforce Management, and Operations to address that finding.
