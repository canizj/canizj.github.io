02 / Google Play

Team Supreme and the Voice of the Customer

Voice of the CustomerSurvey transferPilot scaling

I created and led Team Supreme, a five-person pilot that gave more customers the chance to share feedback after a support interaction. The goal was to make survey handoffs more consistent and give the site a clearer view of the customer experience. I grouped the barriers into process, policy, product, and agent execution. The review covered dropped-call callbacks, knowledge-base exemptions, inconsistent guidance on third-party transfers, and a reporting issue where email transfers could count as phone surveys.

The pilot also brought departments together to review recurring issues and work through fixes jointly. That gave teams a way to resolve issues that had previously been passed between them.

03 / Google Pay

Lean Six Sigma and in-app identity verification

Lean Six SigmaProcess mappingRoot-cause analysis

I mapped the automated verification journey from customer data entry through identity confirmation. I audited cases, interviewed agents across support tiers and other stakeholders, and used the Five Whys to trace recurring errors. Failed verification could prevent customers from using their cards for about two days and drive spikes in calls. Customers who tried to make payments overnight also faced a Tier 2 coverage gap.

The project took about six months from investigation through implementation. I redrew the process and proposed guided in-app steps, including taking a photo, so customers could actively confirm their identity. I submitted the revised map and worked with stakeholders on the final changes.

04 / SOPs & Knowledge Ecosystem

SOPs, product pilots, and coaching

Share product knowledge

I created The Professor to recognize agents who taught customers how to use product features during support calls. I also designed certificates for top performers. I proposed product feature workshops and refresher sessions where Quality Assurance and team leads could revisit app features and knowledge-base guidance with agents who needed extra support.

Test features with Google and learn from customers

In Movie Club and Book Club, we watched a movie or read a book with Google staff while trying product features and giving feedback on what worked and what needed refinement before release. I also ran site pilots brought to us by the vendor manager and managed the 2019 TELUS initiatives and pilots document.

For High-Value User Customer Engagement, I had team members play popular games and join player communities to understand customers firsthand. The program included participation rewards. For the Device Lab, I managed the device budget and selected Android phones from different manufacturers and TV devices so agents could test features on the floor.

Build site-wide coaching and quality practices

In 2017, I designed and presented a Point of Contact (POC) role for the Google support program, drawing on an approved model at the TELUS Guatemala site. I defined how agents would get help when their team lead was unavailable and how the POC would review cases, report on performance, coach agents, and hold calibration meetings. I also estimated the cost and proposed dedicated time away from the queue for those responsibilities.

The original proposal was declined. When I returned to the Google program, the site had adopted a Subject Matter Expert (SME) role that served a similar frontline support function.

I also created the CC 2.0 call checklist so agents could consistently cover the steps QA assessed.

After earning top-performing Team Lead recognition, I became the first Coaching Champion at the site. I ran a special project with the lead account supervisor and coached about 50 agents per week across shifts, including overnight. I focused on agents in the bottom quartile and used a SWOT-based approach to identify strengths to build on, recurring quality gaps, opportunities for improvement, and barriers to progress. I used those findings to guide call scripts, workshops, individual coaching, and performance improvement plans.
