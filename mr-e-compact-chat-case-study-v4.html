01 / AI & AUTOMATION

Mr. E.

AI-assisted architecture for mortgage recruiting.

A proof of concept for candidate research, NMLS identity verification, and CRM data unification, with recruiters reviewing the results before any update.

THE CHALLENGE

Optimizing data aggregation for recruitment.

Mortgage recruiting data is spread across production reports, NMLS records, and the CRM. Comparing it across tabs takes time and leaves room for manual entry errors.

I designed an AI-assisted workflow to reconcile that evidence in one place and give recruiters explicit control over CRM updates.

THE WORKFLOW

Research. Verify. Review.

01 / DATA EXTRACTION & VERIFICATION

Integrating distributed intelligence into a unified, actionable profile.

Automate the synthesis of production figures from Model Match or MMI, NMLS identity evidence, and historical CRM data into one view.

The broader vision. Run automated cross-platform audits, flag conflicting records, and scan entire branch rosters for top-producing loan officers and branch managers whose titles are missing from the CRM.

ROI & business impact. Reduce time spent switching tabs and assembling profiles. Give recruiters better evidence before outreach, with the goal of improving connect-to-meeting conversion and lowering research cost per candidate.

02 / QUALITY ASSURANCE & SYSTEM UPDATE

Maintaining operational integrity and scaling CRM data hygiene.

Generate detailed, standardized CRM notes so recruiters can validate, edit, and approve updates without drafting them from scratch.

The broader vision. Triage new message leads, surface missed calls, and log recruiter feedback. Use that context to suggest the next action and flag stale or incomplete CRM records for review.

ROI & business impact. Reduce routine data cleanup and give recruiters more time for relationship building. Support a growing candidate pipeline without requiring administrative effort to grow at the same rate.

How CRM connections work

Follow Up Boss was used for the proof of concept. Another CRM can support the same workflow if its API exposes the required records and grants the appropriate permissions. A connector authenticates and maps contact IDs, activity and fields to a common format. After recruiter review, it maps approved changes back to the destination and checks the response before reporting a successful write. This preview uses simulated records.

Candidate Research documentation

Identity and role. Read the CRM contact and activity records using authenticated requests. Use the candidate’s NMLS ID to reconcile license, sponsorship and branch registration. Cross-check the title against the company bio or directory.

Production and transactions. The CRM template uses MMI individual and branch roster figures. The separate Model Match integration used OAuth and read tools getOriginator and listLoans. The documented method checks the originator ID, counts loan rows by reporting period, sums volume, and calculates purchase, refi and other shares from loan dollar amounts. The note reports 14-month, 7-month and YTD volume, 14-month units and product mix, plus branch metrics and roster counts for branch managers.

What the numbers mean. Demo 101 shows 30 individual units over 14 months. Demo 102 shows 40 individual units and 100 branch units over 14 months. The handoff reports a successful live Branch Manager draft with a Gemini HTTP 200 response and reconciled Model Match figures. It does not state the transaction count from that live run.

Quality Check documentation

Start from the open contact. The blueprint shows authenticated reads of the contact, notes, and call history. The audit also reads the latest visible conversation from the open timeline.

Reconcile the evidence. Compare tag placement and stage with the profile and latest reply. Compare saved email and phone with the reply and company directory. Check employer against NMLS sponsorship and role against the directory.

Review and approve. The blueprint describes a separate approval step before writing contact edits or a timeline note through the CRM API. A connected version would re-read the contact, preserve existing phone numbers and unrelated tags, and submit only approved changes.

What was tested. The handoff records a successful read-only Branch Manager research run. This Quality Check and edit preview use fictional data. The available records do not confirm a live write for this flow.

Leader Matching documentation

The Phase 1 sanitized study defines five cases and eight partner campaign rows. The audit compares candidate role, county and metro, production windows, product mix and employer restrictions. Protected employer rules take priority. Candidate totals are not proof of self-generated production in a different period.

This prototype compares eight sanitized campaigns locally. The ranking prioritizes eligible or pending candidates, then geography, role and product mix. THE RESULT

Independent Chrome extension proof of concept.

I built and tested a Chrome extension that combines Model Match production data with NMLS identity evidence in an editable CRM note. I also used Gemini with MMI to research and reconcile production figures for the same workflow.

The documented validation covered the read-only research and draft workflow.

How it connects to any CRM

Follow Up Boss was the CRM used in the proof of concept. To connect another CRM, authenticate with its API and map its contact IDs, fields, activity records, and notes to a common format. The same research and review workflow uses that format to prepare a draft and preview changes.

After recruiter approval, the connector maps the approved changes back to the destination fields and submits them through the CRM API. This approach can work with any CRM that exposes the required read and write operations and grants the appropriate permissions.
